Welcome! My name is Xinyi Li. I am currently a third-year Ph.D. candidate at [Stevens Institute of Technology](https://www.stevens.edu/). My advisor is [Prof. Ying Wang](https://www.stevens.edu/profile/ywang6). I received my Master's degree from [Duke University](https://duke.edu/) in 2024.

Research Interests :book:
======

<div class="interests" markdown="1">

* :atom_symbol: **Neutral-Atom Systems**: Hardware-aware compilation, pulse scheduling, atom movement, and resource management.
* :atom_symbol: **Quantum Error Correction & Security**: Atom-loss modeling, decoder vulnerabilities, and fault-tolerant defenses.
* :cloud: **Quantum Cloud Systems**: Multi-tenant virtual QPUs, compilation caching, scheduling, and resource sharing.
* :star: **Quantum Algorithms & ML**: Variational algorithms, QAOA/VQE, hybrid learning, and noise-aware optimization.
